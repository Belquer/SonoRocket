- **Windows (computador que recebe o som do Pro Tools):** [SonoRocket-windows.zip](https://github.com/Belquer/SonoRocket/releases/latest/download/SonoRocket-windows.zip). Extraia, abra o `SonoRocket.exe` e siga o `LEIA-ME.txt`.
- **Mac moderno (plugin no master, Pro Tools com AAX):** [SonoRocket-mac.zip](https://github.com/Belquer/SonoRocket/releases/latest/download/SonoRocket-mac.zip). Veja o [COMO-USAR](https://github.com/Belquer/SonoRocket/blob/main/sonorocket/macos/COMO-USAR.md).

Quem ouve não instala nada: basta abrir o link no celular ou no computador.
